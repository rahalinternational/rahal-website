Our story

Rahal International began in the 1960s as a small family trading business. Over three generations, it has grown organically into a trusted global supplier of fruit and vegetable juice concentrates, purees, and specialty ingredients — without losing the personal, relationship-first approach it was built on.

Today we work as importers, sales agents, and distributors, sourcing from CPG companies, private label co-manufacturers, and co-packers across more than 20 countries. We manage over 5,000 truck and container shipments a year on behalf of more than 200 customers — from the largest CPG companies and bottlers to small startup food brands.

What hasn't changed is how we operate: hands-on, around the clock, and focused on being a supply partner our customers can build a business on.
